7 Days to Choose Your Work
A step-by-step guide to choosing your work
Summary
Over the past 7 days, we’ve explored:
Day 1
Popular Myth: Success comes from discipline.
Overlooked Truth: Success comes from devotion.
Day 2
Popular Myth: If you’ve invested years into a career, it would be foolish to start something new and squander everything you’ve built.
Overlooked Truth: No matter how many years you’ve invested into a career, if it brings you angst, you owe it to yourself to make changes. You deserve peace of mind.
Day 3
Popular Myth: Regret arises from failing to achieve your goals.
Overlooked Truth: Regret arises from not acting on the things that deeply matter to you.
Day 4
Popular Myth: You must quit your job to choose your work.
Overlooked Truth: You can keep your job and choose your work today.
Day 5
Popular Myth: Pursue the things that you’re good at.
Overlooked Truth: Pursue the things that give you energy.
Day 6
Popular Myth: If it can’t be quantified, it doesn’t matter.
Overlooked Truth: The most important things in the world cannot be quantified.
Day 7
Popular Myth: Real success is financial success.
Overlooked Truth: Real success is living a meaningful life that fascinates and challenges you.
But more important than the concepts is the action you take. So ask yourself:
  • What is the one project that keeps calling to you?
  • When will you schedule the first hour to work on it?
  • What is the smallest step you can take today?

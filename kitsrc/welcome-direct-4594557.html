7 Days to Choose Your Work
A step-by-step guide to choosing your work
Have you ever felt the unnerving sensation that, despite appearing successful, something deep down isn’t quite right?
When you look back at your life, will you be satisfied? Or will you have regrets?
First, one small favor: hit reply and send the word “in” to help ensure you receive the upcoming sessions.
This course is based on one short insight each morning at 8 a.m.
1Day 1 of 7: Why Discipline Is Not the Key to Success
2Day 2 of 7: When Does It Make Sense To Start Something New?
3Day 3 of 7: The Truth About Regret
4Day 4 of 7: How to Choose Your Work Today
5Day 5 of 7: How to Overcome the Curse of Competence
6Day 6 of 7: Why You Get Fooled by Numbers
7Day 7 of 7: The Definition of Success
This course builds on the ideas in Choose Your Work. You may have already read the book; if not, that’s fine, you’ll still get the full value here. This course gives you practical actions each day while the book takes you on an in-depth journey to the work you’re meant to do.
What to expect next
This is a 7-day email course. Day 1 arrives tomorrow at 8 a.m.. The next day arrives the morning after you complete the previous day’s activity.
Tomorrow’s email will challenge Myth 1: Success comes from discipline. You’ll discover a sharp insight from a famous opera singer and an unexpected way to win a Nobel Prize.

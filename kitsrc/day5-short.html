How to Overcome the Curse of Competence

Popular Myth

Pursue the things that you’re good at.

Overlooked Truth

Pursue the things that give you energy.
Jeremy seems like he has it all...
Jeremy is a seasoned graphic designer with a polished portfolio, flawless designs, a full calendar, and a very profitable business. On paper, he has reached the pinnacle of his profession.
Alice is just starting out. She has rough edges but is utterly fascinated by her work.
One Friday evening the two met for a drink. Alice asked Jeremy, “Do you ever feel proud of what you’ve made?” Jeremy looked away. “Honestly? Not really. I just feel... tired.”
Their conversation highlights a forgotten truth about your strengths and weaknesses.
A strength is not accurately defined by what you are good at. Rather, a strength is something that makes you feel stronger. It’s an expression of your true self. A weakness makes you feel weaker, even when you are good at it. Skills are transferable. Time is not.
